# Invitation to courts and justice-technology collaborators

**Help test whether source-linked submission review reduces avoidable work.**

Court registries, judicial research teams, court-administration bodies, filing-
system teams, legal researchers and access-to-justice practitioners are welcome
to discuss a carefully scoped evaluation of Court Submission Integrity.

Start with the [fictional demonstration](DEMO.md) and
[capability guide](CAPABILITIES.md). This is an independent, AI-assisted research
alpha directed by Albert Tan Sin Hon, not a court-endorsed or production-ready
service. No institution named in a research source is represented as a partner.

## A proportionate first collaboration

First identify one real operational difficulty and the appropriate court,
proceeding and document type. Review the public fictional examples and agree what
would count as a useful result. No case files, credentials or system access are
needed for this initial conversation.

A subsequent evaluation could use an agreed fictional or expressly cleared set,
labelled by authorised people who did not implement the checks. Record each
reviewer's role, interests, training and assistance. Compare ordinary manual review
with the proposed workflow, measuring false alarms, missed defects, source coverage,
correction effort and total human review time. Predefine acceptance and stop criteria.

Only after the relevant institutional, legal, rights and security approvals would
an in-environment or read-only shadow pilot be considered. Nothing here authorises
real-data processing, production integration, experimental changes to filing
acceptance or research participation without required approvals.

## Useful contributions

- Describe a recurring non-confidential submission or production-index problem.
- Review whether a finding is clear, proportionate and practically correctable.
- Propose fictional good, adverse and genuinely uncertain examples, including
  legitimate alternative arguments and self-represented writing.
- Help define an independent comparison and the errors that would make a tool
  unacceptable, rather than selecting examples merely to demonstrate success.

Discuss scope and reuse terms before substantive contributions. Participation
creates no automatic authorship, payment, revenue share or institutional affiliation.
Existing reserved rights remain unchanged. No licence, commercial obligation,
free unlimited service or commitment to a pilot is granted by this invitation.

## Contact and safe information sharing

For a non-confidential collaboration enquiry, email **travelingbert@gmail.com**
(Albert Tan Sin Hon), with subject **Court Submission Integrity collaboration**.
Include the institution or role, jurisdiction, document type, operational problem
and the proposed evaluation. A short initial note is sufficient.

Public, fictional reproduction reports can use the
[repository issue route](https://github.com/Albertito-AI/court-submission-integrity/issues).
Identify the revision, command, expected behaviour and actual result. Do not include
filings, party names, privileged/closed material, personal data, credentials or
confidential institutional information in an issue or initial email. Agree an
appropriate channel and permissions before sharing any restricted material.

Albert authorised an initial round of non-binding collaboration enquiries on
1 November 2026. Sending or receiving an enquiry does not establish a partnership,
permission to process case data, approval to deploy or a measured benefit. Detailed
